01 // About

PILOT PROFILE

I'm Diego Humerez, an AI and full-stack software engineer based in La Paz, Bolivia. I design and ship AI-powered products — retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, LLM agents and document intelligence — together with the full-stack engineering they need to reach production: .NET, React, TypeScript, Python and FastAPI.

My current flagship is Asesor Legal IA, a production legal-AI platform for Bolivian law: a grounded RAG pipeline over 9 legal codes and 4,170+ articles, where every answer carries citations you can verify against the official source — no hallucinated law.

Before going deep on AI I spent years shipping enterprise software, working remotely with US companies and building SaaS for businesses across Latin America. That mix means I don't just prototype models — I deliver authenticated, containerized, monitored products running on real infrastructure.

Off duty you'll find me building Gunpla, queuing Dota 2, hunting street food or planning the next trip.
